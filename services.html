What We Do & Our Expertise

Ampplify Academy is the Amazon account management service arm of Rakiza Company LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom (128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX). We provide hands-on, done-for-you Amazon Seller account management for sellers on the Amazon USA, Saudi Arabia (KSA), and UAE marketplaces.

Core Offerings

End-to-end Amazon Seller account management: new account setup and reactivation, product and supplier research, listing creation and keyword optimization, A+ Content and storefront design, brand registration, and Sponsored Products/Brands/Display advertising management.

Our Expertise

Our team has 3+ years of hands-on, full-time experience managing real Amazon Seller Central accounts — not just teaching theory. We work directly inside client accounts across multiple marketplaces, handling everything from account health issues to day-to-day advertising optimization.

Benefits of Working With Us

A boutique, specialist-led service rather than a call-center model — every account is handled directly by our team, not outsourced or handed off. You can choose a single one-off service or a full-service plan, with the total cost shown upfront before you commit.
